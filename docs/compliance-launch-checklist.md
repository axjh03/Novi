# Novi compliance launch checklist

The site now has product-specific Privacy, Terms, and Referral Terms pages, plus in-app just-in-time notices before microphone/system-audio capture and camera access. These are implementation drafts, not legal clearance. Confirm the facts below with counsel before broad public sales, especially outside the United States.

- Identify the legal operator of Novi and add its registered name and business mailing address to the notices. Confirm the launch countries, minimum age, governing law, and where users may exercise privacy rights.
- Have Illinois counsel review live interview audio capture/transcription and the participant-consent flow. A user's in-app confirmation does not itself obtain consent from other people or override employer, interviewer, or meeting-platform rules.
- Have counsel classify the camera gaze features and any future voice/speaker identification under Illinois BIPA and relevant state/international biometric laws. Do not add identity matching or biometric templates without a fresh review.
- Verify every privacy statement against production: hosting/log providers, exact account and audit retention, password/account deletion behavior, provider list, and API-key access. Add vendor/subprocessor disclosures and data-processing terms where required.
- Set up a secure referral payout method and accounting process before accepting bank details. Track attribution and cleared payments, issue the promised rewards on schedule, and confirm tax reporting duties with an accountant. Do not collect bank details through ordinary email.
- Use only licensed or original marketing images, GIFs, music, character references, quotes, and voices. The current marketing no longer embeds the *Silicon Valley* still/quote or the externally hosted referral GIF; restore any such material only after obtaining rights.
- Before enabling online checkout or renewals, have counsel review purchase disclosures, refund/cancellation terms, tax handling, consent records, and the cancellation path for each sales jurisdiction.
